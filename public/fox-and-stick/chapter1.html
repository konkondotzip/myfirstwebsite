Chapter 1:
The Gorge Below

Phineas

(Okay… by my count… this dungeon floor… has 46 cedars, 41 maples, 13 birch and 5 beech trees… How does that make any sense?)

Phineas the Delphox was surveying the diversity of tree species in Mystery Dungeons. He wanted to find out what causes the plant diversity to change so drastically in relatively short time spans. His notepad was filled to the brim with notes and bookmarks.

Phineas

(The trees are the same ones from the floor below, but they seem to move around erratically. And just two months ago, there hasn’t been a single cedar tree here, but now, they make up the majority…)

He took careful note of which tree went where on a hand-drawn map. He made sure to tag each and every one of them. But for today, time was running out. The sun was setting, so Phineas took out his Escape Orb and left the dungeon.

Phineas

(I think I’ve gathered enough data to be useful… maybe the professor can make sense of it.)

As Phineas was about to make his way home, which was just a stone’s throw away, he ran into a group of a Toedscruel, a Toxicroak, and their leader, a Grimmsnarl. Phineas tried to avoid them all day, but they were blocking the only path to Kalavena Village, which was on the other side of a bridge.

Phineas

(Damn it, they’re still there…)

The trio turned around and smirked at Phineas. The Toxicroak scoffed at him.

Jack

“Well, if it isn’t Penelope. Finally done counting the trees? Hah!”

Phineas

[I already told you my name was Phineas.]

Jack

“What’s with the sign language? Did we leave you speechless? Come on Penny, say hi.”

Phineas tried to make a move past the three and cross the bridge, but the Toadscruel blocked his path.

Don

“Penny! It’s rude to walk past someone without saying hello!”

Phineas

[I wasn’t planning on doing that.]

Don

“Maybe I’ll make it easier for you…”

The Tentacruel took Phineas’s notebook with one of his tentacles and held it out over the bridge’s reiling.

Don

“It’s like a toll. You can pay by either saying ‘Hello’… or by leaving your notes behind.”

The other two were surrounding Phineas from behind, trapping him.

Don

“So, what will it be, Penelope?”

Phineas

(…)

Jack

“The clock is ticking…”

He taunted him by dropping the notebook a few inches, catching it with another tentacle. The others just laughed.

Phineas

[Stop that! Right now! I won’t bow to your words!]

Don

“Is that your final answer?”

Phineas remained silent.

Don

“Shame… This data might have been useful. Heh, keyword being ‘might’.”

He dropped the notebook into the gorge below. Though, without hesitation, Phineas used his Psychic to catch it. The trio of troublemakers didn’t seem to be very amused by that trick, though. The Grimmsnarl, who stayed on the sidelines until now, just stomped onto the bridge, getting close and personal with Phineas.

Leopold

“Drop it, Penny. You heard him. You have to pay the toll.”

Phineas

[… No.]

The Grimmsnarl grabs Phineas by the scruff of his chest. Despite being taller than the Grimmsnarl, he got overwhelmed by his strength.

Leopold

“Do you want to make it home in one piece or not, Penelope?”

Phineas

(………)

The leader of the group gave a signal to Jack and Don to charge. Phineas screamed.

Phineas

“AAAAARGH!!!”

Phineas dropped the notebook out of fear, mere moments before their attacks connected. Leopold smirked.

Leopold

“There we go. Leave her, boys. She paid the toll.”

The trio made their leave. Don turned around one final time to bark at Phineas.

Don

“Would’ve been easier if you just said hello.”

Phineas

(…)

Phineas

(………)

Phineas didn’t look at them and just watched the notebook fall into the void.

The next day, Phineas built up his courage to retrieve that notebook.

Phineas

(It’s a steep climb… but… what other choice do I really have…?)

He went to the bridge early in the morning, when his bullies were still asleep. If he finds it quickly, he thought, he might avoid them entirely…

Phineas

(I was so stupid… I should have just folded… What was I thinking… Now I have to do all this dangerous… rock climbing…)

He was intimidated by the gorge. It was at least 100 meters deep. Luckily it was the dry season, so his book might have survived the fall. He could always sew the pages back together…

Phineas

(Fuck… Why do I have to have such a feminine voice… I thought it would get deeper as I evolved, but it only got worse… But even when I try to speak, words just won’t come out…)

Several meters down the grueling climb, Phineas could see a bookmark caught in some shrubs. He picked it up from afar using Psychic.

Phineas

(Damn it. The book probably fell way deeper than that…)

Some time later, about two-thirds of the way to the bottom, Phineas spotted the book! It was caught in some kind of vine. He tried to pull on it with Psychic, but the vine was holding it tightly.

Phineas

(Damn. Looks like I’ll have to pull it out by hand…)

Upon closer inspection, the vine was way stiffer than expected. It was more like a tree branch of some kind, poking out of a bush, wrapped IN a vine. Phineas grabbed the branch and tried to pull it out.

Phineas

(This thing is really in there, isn’t it…)

From somewhere, a voice popped up. It was faint, but definitely not his imagination.

???

“Wait wait wait. Did I just hear someone?! Was I FINALLY FOUND?!”

Phineas was startled by this and dropped the branch, including the book.

Phineas

(What the hell was that?! Shit… I dropped it!!! Why am I always so damn clumsy?!)

With his arms and legs starting to give out, Phineas climbed the rest of the way, to the bottom of the gorge. The book was still in one piece, with the branch and vines around it.

Phineas

(Well, phew… finally. Thank heavens, the book looks fine…)

He tried to grab the branch and the book to separate them, but got interrupted by that voice again.

???

“Wait! WAIT!! Don’t leave me! I’m begging you!!!”

Phineas

(What the fuck?! Who said that?!)

???

“Look down, you idiot! I’m the thing you’re holding!”

Phineas

(The book?!)

???

“No! The thing intertwined with the book!”

Phineas

(The branch…?)

???

“Now you’re getting it. Now PLEASE, listen.”

Rudolph

“My name is Rudolph. I used to be a Serperior, but I got transformed into this goddamn branch. I lost track of time, it felt like I’ve been like this for eternity. Please, you must help me!”

Phineas

(What?! Hang on, let me…)

Phineas unwrapped the branch from the book by removing the vine. What he was talking to was not just a branch, but some kind of wooden wand. He could tell because one end was glowing bright blue, while the other end was quite fancy and adorned with ivy leaves.
Phineas

(Your name is Rudolph? But I’m holding a stick.)

Rudolph

“Maybe you didn’t hear me the first time. I got TRANSFORMED, smart guy! And I’m a wand, not a stick!”

Phineas

(Uhm. I guess. So like. Thanks for catching my book?)

Rudolph

“So it IS your book! Oh, lucky day, lucky day! You dropping your book into this chasm is the reason I got saved!”

Phineas

(Uh. I suppose that’s true. …My name is Phineas, by the way.)

Rudolph

“Phineas! Oh… what pleasure to hear another lad’s voice after all this time…”

Phineas

(Another… lad’s voice…? So, how long have you been down here?)

Rudolph

“I have no damn clue. What date is it?”

Phineas

(Well… 31st of August, Acceleration Era 52.)

Rudolph

“Did you just say fifty-TWO? I’ve been here for over a year!!! Over a year with just me and my thoughts!”

Phineas

(Dude, calm down for a second! It wasn’t me who threw you down here!)

Rudolph

“I sure HOPE you didn’t! Now let’s get out of here!”

Phineas

(Uh. Easier said than done. I’m kind of tired from climbing…)

The gorge had no easy way in or out, other than climbing. The only other way out was by following downhill, where the gorge eventually meets with a river.

Rudolph

“Then start walking, dude! I’d do it myself if I had legs!”

Phineas

(Alright alright, yikes!)

Phineas took his book and stuck Rudolph in his tail, which felt like the most natural place to put it, given he’s a Delphox. He started walking along the gorge.

Phineas

(So like, can you just hear my thoughts? Since I wasn’t opening my mouth.)

Rudolph

“I guess!”

Phineas

(And uhm, can you see or hear anything else?)

Rudolph

“Do I look like I have eyes or ears? Or a mouth, for that matter?”

Phineas

(Then how did you know you were holding a book…?)

Rudolph

“I don’t know how, but I can talk to all the other stuff that everyone throws down this gorge. Though they aren’t really talkative, since they’re, y’know, objects…”

Phineas

(Huh? So the book fell on you, and you just kind of… talked to it?)

Rudolph

“I guess ‘talk’ is the wrong word for it.”

Phineas

(Okay… Did you read what was written inside it?)

Rudolph

“Dude, all that was in there were scribbles and tally marks next to pictures of trees.”

Phineas

(Hey, my handwriting isn’t THAT bad…!)

And so, Phineas carried Rudolph all the way out of the gorge. What a strange fellow this stick turned out to be…
