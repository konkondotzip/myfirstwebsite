How it works

You contact me via social media (Bluesky preferred) and describe what you want. Send as many relevant pictures, ideas, descriptions, etc. as possible. If I accept the commission, you will pay up front (PayPal only). If I don't finish the commission within an agreed upon time frame (eg. one month), I will give you a full refund.
